FAQ

Questions? We’ve got answers

Everything you need to know about plans, billing and getting started.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for up to three people, with no credit card required. Upgrade any time when your team grows.

Can I change plans later?

Of course. Upgrade or downgrade from your billing settings at any time. Changes apply immediately and we prorate the difference.

How does the free trial work?

Paid plans include a 14-day free trial with every feature unlocked. If you don’t add a payment method, your workspace simply moves to the free plan.

Can I import from another tool?

Yes. One-click importers bring in projects, tasks and comments from the most popular tools, and a CSV import covers everything else.

Is my data secure?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily and hosted in SOC 2 certified data centres. Business plans add SSO and audit logs.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes. Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off any paid plan. Contact us with proof of status to apply.
