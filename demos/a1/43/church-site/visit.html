First visit

Good to know

What people often ask before their first Sunday.

What should I wear?

Whatever you are comfortable in. You will see jeans and suits.

What happens for my children?

Children start in the service, then go to kids’ church with checked leaders. You collect them at the end.

Will I be asked to give money?

No. Giving is for regular members; guests are our guests.

Is the building accessible?

Yes. There is step-free access at the side door, an accessible toilet and a hearing loop.
