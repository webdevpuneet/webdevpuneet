Our story

Built by lifters, for everyone

IRONHOUSE opened in 2016 in a converted warehouse with six racks and one rule: nobody trains alone. Today 900 members share the floor, from first-timers on their first week to athletes training for race day.

Every class is coached and every programme scales to your level, so you can start where you are and get stronger every single week.

FAQ

Before you start

Free trials, beginners, freezing your membership and the changing rooms.

Yes. Seven days of unlimited classes and open gym, no card needed.

I’m a complete beginner. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Every class is scaled, and your first session starts with an induction.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, for up to three months a year, for holidays or injury.

Do you have showers and lockers?

Yes, with towels, lockers and hair dryers included.
