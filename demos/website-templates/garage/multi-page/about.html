Our story

A garage built on plain speaking

Dave Marsh opened Redline in 2005 after years in a dealership, where he felt customers were never told what their cars really needed. He wanted to run a garage where the advice is honest and the prices are clear.

Twenty years later the team has grown to eleven, with four ramps and a customer waiting area, and Dave still tests cars every morning before the phone starts ringing.
