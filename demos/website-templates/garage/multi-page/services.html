Good to know

Questions about prices and repairs

What drivers ask before they book.

Do you give a price before starting work?

Always. You get a written estimate before any work starts, and if we find anything else we phone you first and wait for your say-so.

What happens if my car fails its MOT?

We explain exactly what failed and quote the repair. If you leave the car with us and the repairs are done within ten working days, the partial retest is free.

Will servicing here affect my manufacturer warranty?

No. We use parts of manufacturer quality and record the service in your book, so your new-car warranty stays valid.

Do you offer a courtesy car or a lift?

We give a free lift into Leeds centre for jobs that take over two hours, and courtesy cars are available for longer repairs when booked ahead.

How long is your warranty?

Twelve months or 12,000 miles on every repair and every part we fit, whichever comes first.
