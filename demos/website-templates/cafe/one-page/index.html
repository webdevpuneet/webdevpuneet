Why Bean & Bloom

A café that takes its time

The small things that make a morning stop worth the detour.

Roasted in-house

We roast every Tuesday in small batches from four farms we have visited, so your beans are never more than a week old.

Baked before sunrise

Croissants, brownies and sourdough come out of our oven by 6.30 am, seven days a week.

Order ahead

Order online, pick up in about ten minutes and walk straight past the queue.
