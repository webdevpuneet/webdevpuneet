Good to know

Dogs, wifi and allergens

A few things people ask before they visit.

Are dogs welcome?

Yes. Well-behaved dogs are welcome inside and on the patio, and there is a water bowl by the door.

Is there wifi, and can I work here?

Free wifi and plenty of sockets. Laptops are welcome all day, with a request to share the big table at busy times.

Do you have plant milks and gluten-free options?

Oat, soy and almond milk are free of charge. Several pastries are gluten free or vegan, marked on the menu, and the team can answer allergen questions.

How does ordering ahead work?

Add items to your order, check out and tell us a pickup time. Your drink is made when you arrive, and the order waits on the counter.

Can I book for a group?

For groups of eight or more email us a week ahead and we will reserve tables and prepare a pastry platter.
