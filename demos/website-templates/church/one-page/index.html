Your first Sunday

What to expect

Services last about an hour. Children are welcome in every service.

  1. 9:15 am

    Coffee and a welcome

    Our welcome team meets you at the door with coffee and answers to any questions.

  2. 9:30 am

    Worship together

    Songs, a reading and a short talk about life and faith, in plain language.

  3. 10:00 am

    Kids’ church

    Children aged 3 to 11 go to their own groups with our checked leaders.

  4. 10:30 am

    Stay and chat

    Tea, cake and time to meet people. No pressure, ever.
