Working together

Before you get in touch

A few answers to save us both some time.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Web apps, shops, dashboards and APIs, from a first prototype to rebuilding something that has outgrown its code. I work best with teams of two to twenty people.

Are you available for new work?

I have space from January. Tell me what you are building and when you need it, and I will say honestly whether it fits.

How do you work and charge?

Fixed-scope projects get a fixed price after a short discovery call. Longer work is billed by the week, with a clear plan and a demo at the end of each one.

Do you work with teams in other time zones?

Yes. I am based in Berlin and overlap comfortably with Europe and the Americas, and I write things down so asynchronous work stays smooth.

Can you join an existing codebase?

Often, yes. I start by reading the code, running the tests and writing down what I find, so we agree on a plan before anything changes.
