FAQ

Before you arrive

Check-in, breakfast, children and getting here from the airport.

What time is check-in?

From 3 pm, and check-out is until 11 am. We store luggage before and after your stay.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, breakfast is included in every rate and served until noon.

Do you allow children?

Children over 12 are welcome; the hotel is designed as a quiet retreat.

Can you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. Faro airport is 45 minutes away and we can book a private transfer for you.
