Our story

A clinic built around calm visits

Dr. Maya Chen opened Willow & Paw in 2014 after years in a busy city hospital, with one aim: a clinic where pets are not scared and owners are never rushed.

Ten years later the team has grown to nine, with its own lab, X-ray and a quiet cat-only waiting room, and Maya still sees patients every day.
