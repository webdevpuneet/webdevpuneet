Good to know

Questions about costs and care

What most owners ask before their first visit.

Do you give a price before treatment?

Always. You get a written estimate before anything is done, and we explain each line. If a treatment needs to change, we call you first.

Can I pay in instalments?

Our wellness plans spread the cost of routine care into a monthly payment. For larger bills we can talk about a payment plan before treatment starts.

Do you see exotic pets?

We see dogs, cats, rabbits and guinea pigs. For birds, reptiles and other exotics we will refer you to a specialist we trust.

How quickly can my sick pet be seen?

Call before noon and we will see your pet the same day during opening hours. Out of hours, call our emergency line.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Any vaccination records, medicines your pet takes and, for a sick pet, a note of the symptoms. A stool sample helps for tummy problems.
