The redesign that almost sank the company

A founder tells the story of a rebuild that went wrong, what it cost and the one decision that saved it.

    About this episode

    Eighteen months into a full rebuild, the team had spent most of their money and shipped nothing. This week, the founder explains how it happened, how it felt and what they changed.

      In this episode

      We talk about the choices that matter when a project drifts.

      • How a “quick rebuild” became a year and a half
      • The meeting where everything changed
      • Shipping in small pieces instead of one big launch
