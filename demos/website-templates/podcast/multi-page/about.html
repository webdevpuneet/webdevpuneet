Be a guest, or a sponsor

How we pick guests and work with brands.

How do you choose guests?

We look for people who built something real and will talk honestly about the hard parts, not just the wins.

Can I pitch myself or a client?

Yes. Send a short note with the story you want to tell and why our listeners would care.

Do you take sponsors?

One sponsor per episode, read by the hosts, and only for products we would use ourselves.

How long is an episode?

Usually 45 to 60 minutes, with chapters so you can jump to the part you want.
