Redesigning a booking flow in four weeks

The research, the sketches and the results behind a flow that cut drop-off by a third.

Alex RiveraProduct designer

October 7, 2026·8 min read

In this article

    The problem

    One in three visitors who started booking left before the last step. The team had theories but no evidence, so we began by watching real people use the flow.

      What we found

      Three patterns explained most of the drop-off.

      • The calendar hid available times behind a second tap
      • Prices changed between steps
      • The form asked for information we already had

      The result

      Four weeks later the new flow was live. Drop-off fell by 34%, support questions about prices nearly disappeared and the team kept the weekly testing habit.
