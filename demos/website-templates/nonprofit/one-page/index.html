Our story

Ten years of planting forests

Keep scrolling to follow GreenRoots from a single village nursery to millions of trees.

  1. 2016

    The first nursery

    One village, one nursery and a thousand native seedlings planted by neighbours.

  2. 2018

    Local teams grow

    Women-led planting teams are paid fairly to grow and protect every tree.

  3. 2020

    A million trees in sight

    Ten villages and the first 100,000 trees, with survival checked every season.

  4. 2022

    Schools join in

    Pupils plant and track trees near their schools, learning how forests return.

  5. 2024

    Four countries

    The programme reaches 38 villages in 4 countries, with 1,100 local jobs.

  6. 2026

    2.4 million trees

    A decade on, forests are returning and 92% of every donation goes to projects.
