Our story

How we found each other

Eight years, two cities and one very nervous question.

  1. 2019 · London

    A seat at the same table

    A friend’s birthday dinner, two empty chairs side by side, and a conversation that lasted until the restaurant closed.

  2. 2021 · The Alps

    Our first big adventure

    A week of hiking, one wrong turn and a sunrise we still talk about. We knew we made a good team.

  3. 2025 · Sintra

    The question

    Daniel planned a picnic on the hill where we will get married. Maya said yes before he finished asking.

  4. 2027 · With you

    Now, the party

    All that is missing is the people we love. We cannot wait to celebrate with you.

Wedding day schedule

How do I get to the venue?

Quinta da Serra is 30 minutes from Lisbon. A shuttle leaves the Sintra train station at 2:00 pm and returns at midnight and 2 am.

Where should I stay?

We have partner rates at three hotels in Sintra. Mention our names when you book to get the discount.

What should I wear?

Summer formal. The ceremony is on grass, so block heels or flats are a good idea.

Can I bring a plus one?

If your invitation includes a guest, their name is on it. Please list everyone attending in your RSVP.
