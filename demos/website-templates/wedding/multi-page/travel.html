Travel

Getting there

Flights, the shuttle, parking and the weather.

Which airport should I fly into?

Lisbon airport is 40 minutes from Sintra by taxi or about an hour by metro and train.

When does the shuttle run?

From Sintra station at 2:00 pm, and back to the station at midnight and 2 am.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, there is free parking for 40 cars at Quinta da Serra.

What will the weather be like?

Warm days around 26°C and cooler evenings, so bring a light layer for the night.
