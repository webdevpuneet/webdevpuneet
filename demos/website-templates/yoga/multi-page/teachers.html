Our story

A studio built for slowing down

Maya Lindqvist opened Stillwater in 2015 in a former printing workshop, with fourteen mats and a belief that yoga should feel welcoming, not intimidating.

Today the studio runs over forty classes a week with four teachers, still keeps classes small, and still has a pot of tea waiting after every session.
