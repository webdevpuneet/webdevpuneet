The studio

A space designed for calm

Natural light, warm wooden floors and plants in every corner. We keep classes small so every student gets attention.

  • Small classes

    No more than 16 students, so teachers can offer hands-on adjustments.

  • Everything provided

    Mats, blocks, straps and blankets are included, along with filtered water and tea.

  • Showers and lockers

    Warm showers, towels to hire and lockers, so you can come straight from work.
