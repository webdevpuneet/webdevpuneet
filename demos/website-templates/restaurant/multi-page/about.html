Our story

A family kitchen since 2014

Rita and Tomás opened Olive & Ember in a former bakery in Alfama, with one wood oven and twelve seats. The oven is still the heart of the kitchen.

  • Local first

    Vegetables, fish and meat from small producers within 100 km.

  • Cooked over fire

    Almost everything passes over oak embers before it reaches you.

  • Family run

    Three generations work in the kitchen and on the floor.
