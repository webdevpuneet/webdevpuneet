    Privacy policy

    Last updated 24 October 2026

    This policy explains what information Olive & Ember collects when you use this website, how we use it and the choices you have. It is sample wording: replace it with a policy that matches what your business really does.

    Information we collect

    We collect the information you give us directly, such as your name, email address and message when you fill in a form or contact us.

    We also collect basic technical information automatically when you visit the site.

    • Details you send us through forms
    • Messages, bookings and enquiries
    • Device, browser and page-view information

    How we use your information

    We use your information to reply to you, to provide the services you ask for and to improve this website. We do not sell your personal data.

    • To answer enquiries and confirm bookings
    • To send messages you have asked for
    • To understand which pages are useful

    Cookies and analytics

    We use a small number of cookies to make the site work and to measure how it is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

      Who we share it with

      We share information only with the service providers who help us run the website, such as hosting, email and form services, and only as far as they need to do their job. We may also share it when the law requires.

        Your choices and rights

        You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to stop using it for certain purposes. Contact us and we will help.

        • Ask for a copy of your data
        • Correct anything that is wrong
        • Ask us to delete your information

        Changes to this policy

        If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top of this page.

          Questions about your data?

          Write to us at privacy@webdevpuneet.com and we will reply within five working days.
