Good to know

Allergies, diets and more

Tell us when you book and the kitchen will plan around you.

Do you cater for vegetarians and vegans?

Yes. Around half the small plates are vegetarian, and the tasting menu has a vegetarian version. Vegan dishes are available on request.

Can you handle allergies?

We can adapt most dishes for common allergies. Please mention them when you book so the kitchen can prepare.

Are children welcome?

Of course. We have high chairs and a small children’s plate of grilled chicken and potatoes.

Do you change the menu often?

Every week, following what the market and our farmers have at their best.
