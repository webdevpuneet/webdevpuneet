Good to know

Gig questions

The things people ask us before a show.

What time do doors open?

Doors open one hour before the listed start time. Support starts about 30 minutes later, and Velvet Static go on stage around 90 minutes after doors.

Can children come to the shows?

Most shows are all ages, but some venues have their own age limits. Under-16s must be with an adult. The age limit is shown on each ticket page.

Can I get a refund if I cannot go?

Tickets are refundable through the ticket seller up to 14 days before the show. After that you can transfer your ticket to a friend for free.

Will there be a show in my city?

We add dates all the time. Join the newsletter for presale access, or ask your local promoter to get in touch with our booking agent.
