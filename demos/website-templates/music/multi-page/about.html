The band

Four friends, one sound

We met in a rehearsal room in Lisbon in 2019 and have not stopped since.

  • Inês Duarte

    Vocals and synths

    Writes the songs on a battered keyboard on the last tram home.

  • Tomás Reis

    Guitar

    Builds walls of sound from three pedals and a vintage amp.

  • Rui Almeida

    Bass

    The calm one. Plays the same two-note groove for eight minutes and makes it work.

  • Carla Mendes

    Drums

    Hits harder than anyone in the building and smiles through all of it.
