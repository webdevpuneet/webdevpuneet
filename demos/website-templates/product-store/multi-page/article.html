How to choose headphones for your commute

Noise cancelling, comfort and battery life: what matters on a train, a bus or a bike, and what does not.

Jonas WeberAudio engineer

October 11, 2026·6 min read

In this article

    Start with where you listen

    A noisy train needs strong noise cancelling. A quiet bike lane needs awareness of the traffic around you. Decide where you spend most of your time before you compare any specifications.

      What to compare

      Ignore the biggest numbers and look at the ones that change your day.

      • Comfort over a full hour
      • Battery life for your week
      • Microphone quality for calls
      • A fit that stays put when you move

      Our recommendation

      For most commuters, over-ear headphones with good noise cancelling and a 30-hour battery are the most comfortable choice. If you walk or cycle, a lighter pair of earbuds with a transparency mode is safer and easier to carry.
