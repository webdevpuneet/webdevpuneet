Introducing live reports

Dashboards that update as your team works: no spreadsheets and no Friday afternoon status updates.

Noah ReyesHead of Product

October 9, 2026·5 min read

In this article

    Why we built it

    Every Friday, someone on every team spent an hour copying numbers into a status update. We watched it happen for a month, then decided it should not be a human job.

    Live reports read straight from your boards and roadmaps, so the numbers are right whenever you open them.

      What it does

      Pick a team, a time range and the metrics you care about. The report updates as work moves, and you can share a link that always shows the latest.

      • Updates as tasks change
      • Share a link or a PDF
      • Compare this week with last week

      What is next

      Next we will add goals and forecasts, so a report can answer the question that actually matters: will we make it on time? Tell us what you would like to see.
