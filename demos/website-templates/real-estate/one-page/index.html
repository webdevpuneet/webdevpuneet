FAQ

Questions answered

Everything you need to know before you sell or buy.

How much does a valuation cost?

It’s free and comes with no obligation. We visit your home and give you a written price range within 48 hours.

What are your fees?

A fixed fee agreed up front, payable only when your sale completes. No sale, no fee.

How long does it take to sell?

Our homes are under offer in 21 days on average, and most sales complete within 12 weeks.

Do you help with mortgages?

Yes. Our independent mortgage adviser compares the whole market to find the best deal for you.
