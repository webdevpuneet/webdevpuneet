Why most rebrands fail in the first year

A new logo is the easy part. What the teams that get it right do before, during and after the launch.

Priya NairStrategy director

October 12, 2026·6 min read

In this article

    The logo is not the brand

    A rebrand fails when the new identity is treated as a paint job. The visual change lands on day one; the change in how the company behaves takes a year.

      What the best teams do

      They agree what is changing and why before anyone opens a design file.

      • Write down the three things the brand must say
      • Bring customer-facing teams in early
      • Plan the first ninety days after launch

      Measure what matters

      Track recognition, consistency and the questions customers ask, not just applause on launch day. A rebrand is finished when the new story is the one people tell.

        “A brand is a promise you keep, not a logo you launch.”
