Treatment FAQ

Before your treatment

Everything you need to know about plans, billing and getting started.

Does whitening damage teeth?

No. Professional whitening with custom trays is safe for enamel. Some people feel short-term sensitivity, which fades within days.

Am I suitable for aligners?

Most adults with crowding or gaps are. A free 3D scan shows your result before you decide.

Do implants hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthetic, and most patients say it is easier than an extraction.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes. Treatments over £500 can be spread over 6–24 months with 0% finance.
