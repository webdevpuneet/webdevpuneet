Admissions

Joining Brookfield

Four simple steps from first visit to first day.

  1. Visit us

    Join a tour on a Tuesday or Thursday morning.

  2. Apply

    Apply through the council by 15 January for Reception.

  3. Hear back

    Offers are sent on 16 April each year.

  4. Settle in

    Home visits and stay-and-play sessions in July.

Common questions

Admissions and the school day.

Which area do you take pupils from?

Most places go to children living closest to the school, after siblings and looked-after children.

Can my child join mid-year?

Yes, if a year group has space. Contact the office to check.

How do you support children with SEND?

Our SENCo works with families and teachers on a plan for every child who needs extra support.

Do you offer wraparound care?

Breakfast club from 7:45 am and after-school clubs until 5:30 pm.
