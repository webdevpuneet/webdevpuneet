The school year

A year at Brookfield

Keep scrolling to see the moments every child looks forward to, from the first day to the summer fair.

  1. September

    Welcome week

    New children settle in with buddies, and families meet their teachers over coffee.

  2. October

    Harvest festival

    Pupils bring food they have grown and share songs with the whole school.

  3. December

    Winter concert

    Every class performs, from the youngest singers to the school orchestra.

  4. February

    Science week

    Experiments, a visiting scientist and a school-wide invention challenge.

  5. May

    Sports day

    Races, relays and team games, with parents cheering from the field.

  6. July

    Summer fair

    Stalls, music and a leavers assembly to close the year together.

For parents

Admissions & the school day

The questions parents ask us most.

?How do I apply for a place?
Apply through your local council for Reception. For other year groups, contact the school office about spaces.
?What are the school hours?
Doors open at 8:40 am and the day ends at 3:15 pm. Breakfast and after-school clubs run from 7:45 am to 5:30 pm.
?Do you provide lunches?
Yes. Hot lunches are cooked on site every day, with vegetarian and allergy-friendly options.
?How do you support children with SEND?
Our SENCo works with families and teachers on a plan for every child who needs extra support.
?Is there a uniform?
Yes, a simple navy uniform. Pre-loved items are free from our parent association.
?Can we visit before applying?
Of course. We run tours most Tuesday and Thursday mornings. Book a place below.
