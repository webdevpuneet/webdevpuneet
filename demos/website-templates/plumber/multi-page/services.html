Good to know

Questions about prices and visits

What homeowners ask before they book.

Do you give a price before starting work?

Always. You get a fixed written price before any work starts, and if we find anything else we phone you first and wait for your say-so.

Are your engineers Gas Safe registered?

Yes. Every engineer who works on gas appliances is on the Gas Safe Register, and they show you their ID card when they arrive.

How quickly can you come in an emergency?

Most emergency call-outs in our core areas arrive within an hour, day or night. Use the area checker for the usual time in your postcode.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

Emergency call-outs start from £95, which includes the first hour of work. For planned jobs, the survey is free and the quote is fixed.

What does your guarantee cover?

Every repair and every part we fit is guaranteed for 12 months, with a free return visit if anything is not right.
