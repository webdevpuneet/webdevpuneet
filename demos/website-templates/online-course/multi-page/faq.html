FAQ

Questions about the course

Everything you need to know about plans, billing and getting started.

Do I need any experience?

No. The course starts from the basics; all you need is a computer and the free version of Figma.

How much time does it take?

Around 6–8 hours a week for eight weeks. Lessons are recorded, so you can learn at your own pace.

What if it’s not for me?

You’re covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of completion you can add to LinkedIn.
