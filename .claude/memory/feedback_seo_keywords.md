---
name: SEO focus on content not meta keywords
description: Do not focus on meta keywords tag — focus on content inside the page (headings, body text, FAQs, schema)
type: feedback
---

Do not spend effort expanding or optimising the `keywords` meta tag. Google has ignored it since 2009 and other engines treat it as a spam signal when overloaded.

**Why:** keyword rankings come from the words naturally present in title, H1, H2s, body text, FAQs, and schema — not from the meta keywords list.

**How to apply:** when improving SEO for any tool page, focus on: rich body content, well-structured headings, detailed FAQs, proper schema markup (HowTo, FAQPage, SoftwareApplication), and comparison/alternative sections. Keep meta keywords to a short list of 8–12 primary terms at most, or skip expanding them entirely.
