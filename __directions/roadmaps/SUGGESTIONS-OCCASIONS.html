How to use this

P1 global or near-global, huge post volume, every business can use it: have a template ready every year. P2 big in one region, faith or season, or strong for selling: build next. P3 industry or awareness days: cover with a reusable template rather than one each. Have a template already exists.

Dates: dates marked ~ follow a lunar or lunisolar calendar (Islamic, Hindu, Chinese, Jewish, Buddhist) or are announced late (Prime Day, Super Bowl) and can move by a day or more; written from memory, so check each one against an official calendar before building or scheduling. Build each template about 3 weeks before its date so it can be indexed and used in time.

Social post templates already built for occasions: Christmas Greeting, New Year, Thanksgiving, Halloween Sale, Valentine's Day, Diwali, Holiday Sale (Black Friday), plus general ones that help (Thank You, Save the Date, Countdown, Discount Badge). Where an occasion also needs an email, note it in SUGGESTIONS-EMAIL.html.

P3 Industry and awareness days

Hundreds of these exist. Cover them with the reusable Awareness Day and Industry Day templates below instead of one each; the full list is in By industry.

Reusable social templates that cover the whole world

About 195 countries and thousands of local festivals cannot each get their own template. Five flexible templates cover most of them, and each can get its own SEO page per big occasion later.

  1. National / Independence Day: country name, date, flag colours as 2–3 editable stripes or a wave, "Happy Independence Day" or "National Day" text. Covers every country in the list below. Motion: a flag that waves, fireworks that burst.
  2. Festival greeting: greeting, festival name, a motif picked from a set (lamp, lantern, crescent, kite, colour powder, candle, flower) and a palette. Covers regional festivals without their own template.
  3. Holiday hours / We're closed: works for Christmas, Eid, Diwali, Lunar New Year, national holidays.
  4. Awareness Day: ribbon or icon in the cause colour (pink for breast cancer, green for mental health), the day's name and one fact or call to action.
  5. Industry Day: "Happy Nurses Day", "Happy Programmers' Day": a role icon, a thank-you line and an optional team photo.
