How to use this

Each idea has a status: Suggested (not started), Building (in progress) or Done (shipped, kept here as a record). Newest first inside each group.

Rule for what gets added: an idea goes here only if it fills a real gap (something visitors look for that the site does not have, or a workaround a current template forces). Ideas for other areas live in their own files; start from the Suggestions index.

Design and animation bar for every new template (decided 2026-11-02):

  • Creative, great-looking design: its own layout and a clear idea, not a recolour of an existing template. Check it at every post size (square, portrait, Story with its safe areas) and with long and short text.
  • Built to animate on export: the video export (MP4 / WebM, in render.js) animates every [data-edit] text slot and [data-image] photo in reading order (rise, fade or pop). Anything meant to animate must be one of those slots, and the slots must be ordered so the reveal reads well (headline, then detail, then the call to action). Decorative shapes are not animated today.
  • Check the video: export the animated video once per new template and watch it before calling the template done.

Idea to make exports feel richer: let a template mark decorative parts as animatable (a data-anim hook) so shapes, badges, ratings and bars move too: stars filling, a progress bar growing, numbers counting up, a countdown ticking, underlines drawing. Countdown, 5-star review, big stat, flash sale and pricing card would benefit first.

For each template: curated, polished, non-generic SEO text (written for that one template, never a pattern with the name swapped; see 06-QUALITY-BARS.txt) within the length limits, preview image, category page entry, sitemap and llms.txt. Check search demand first: "[type] instagram post template".
