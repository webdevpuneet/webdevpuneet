VISION AND POSITIONING
Last updated 2026-10-28

ONE SENTENCE
  webdevpuneet.com gives people professional web design ideas and visual assets they can see,
  touch, learn from and use: templates, sections, snippets, playgrounds and tools that make the
  craft of the web understandable, whether they then build it themselves, with AI, or with Puneet.

WE ARE NOT COMPETING WITH AI
  AI can write code on request. What it cannot do well is show someone a hundred finished,
  tasteful, tested options side by side, let them poke at real working examples, and teach them
  *why* something looks and behaves the way it does. That is our job:

  1. Inspiration you can see.  Real, finished designs (website templates, sections, emails, social
     posts, snippets) with live previews and demos, not a blank prompt box.
  2. Learning you can touch.   Playgrounds and editable snippets: change a value, break it,
     understand it. Every page explains what is going on in plain language.
  3. Assets you can trust.      Professional, accessible, responsive, SEO-ready code that holds up
     in a real project. Exported as HTML, ZIP, React, Vue, Next.js or forked into My Code.
  4. AI as a partner, not a rival. Every template and snippet page carries a ready-made AI prompt
     so users can take our starting point into ChatGPT / Claude / Copilot and extend it. We are
     the best starting point for AI work, and the best check on what AI produces.
  5. A human when you need one. When a project needs judgement, polish or delivery, the user can
     hire Puneet (/puneet/). See 03-LEADS-HIRE-ME.txt.

WHO IT IS FOR
  - Beginners and students learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript and frameworks (playgrounds, snippets).
  - Designers and developers who want a fast, good-looking starting point (templates, sections).
  - Small businesses, creators and marketers who need a site, an email or a social post today
    (website builder, email and social templates).
  - People already using AI who want better inputs and a way to check outputs.
  - Clients who want it done for them (Hire Me).

WHAT MAKES A PAGE "OURS"
  - It shows something real and finished, not a placeholder.
  - It teaches: what it is, how it works, when to use it, how to change it.
  - It is free to use, with no sign-up and no watermark in exports.
  - It is A-grade on SEO, accessibility and performance (06-QUALITY-BARS.txt).
  - It offers a next step: edit, export, fork, ask AI, or hire Puneet.

SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
  - Search traffic to template, snippet, playground and tool pages keeps growing.
  - People stay, edit and export (engagement, exports, forks to My Code).
  - A steady flow of qualified Hire Me enquiries from the pages that show the most intent.
  - The library is known as the place to find and learn real, modern web UI.
