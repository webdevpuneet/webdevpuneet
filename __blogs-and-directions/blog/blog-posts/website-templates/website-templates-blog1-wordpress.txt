<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How the cart works (and what that means for you)</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>All the shop pages share one small cart that lives in the visitor&#8217;s own browser. That is why the template works on any cheap static host with no database, no login and no monthly fee. It also means a few honest limits you should know before you launch:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The cart, wishlist and order history belong to one browser, so they do not follow a shopper to another device.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>There is no stock control, tax calculation or order management. Orders reach you through the form, WhatsApp or payment link you set up.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Promo codes are checked on the page, so anyone can read them in the source. Check them again when you process the order.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>It is a great fit for small shops, makers, merchandise, one-product launches and order-by-message businesses. For hundreds of products and automatic inventory, use a hosted shop platform and keep this design as your front end.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Open the template.</strong> Go to the <a href="https://webdevpuneet.com/website-builder/templates/online-store/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Online Store template page</a> and press the button to open it in the builder. Choose the multi-page version to get all the pages.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<li><strong>Set your brand in the Theme tab.</strong> Pick a colour preset or type exact brand, background, text and surface colours, choose a heading and body font pair, and set the corner radius. One change restyles every section on every page, so the shop looks like one brand in a minute.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Replace the logo and the top bar.</strong> In the header section, type your shop name and pick an icon, or upload a logo image. Edit the top bar message ("Free delivery over $150") and its small links.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Rebuild the mega menu.</strong> Add up to six menu items. Each can have three link columns written as simple "Label | URL" lines and a featured card with a photo, tag, title and button. Leave the first column empty and an item becomes a plain link such as Sale. Point links at filtered shop pages like <code>shop.html?cat=Lighting</code>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Add your products.</strong> On the Shop page edit the product list: photo, name, category, price, old price, colours (comma separated), rating, date added and sold out or in stock. Categories, colour chips and the price limits in the filters are created from your products automatically. Do the same for the product page and the strips.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Set shipping and promo codes.</strong> In the cart and checkout sections set the free-shipping amount, the flat shipping cost, up to four shipping methods and your promo codes (written as <code>WELCOME10 | 10</code> for 10% off).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Choose how orders reach you.</strong> In the Checkout section pick form service, WhatsApp or payment link, then paste the form address, WhatsApp number or payment URL. Add your payment options and your order-number prefix.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Tune the product page.</strong> Switch sizes on if you sell clothing, tag each gallery photo with a colour so the swatches switch the picture, edit the trust badges and details, and turn the wishlist heart or sticky bar on or off.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Write your own words.</strong> Click any text in the preview to edit it. Rewrite the hero headline, the reasons to shop, the reviews and the help answers so they sound like you. Wrap words in asterisks to highlight them in your brand colour.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Add or remove sections.</strong> Drop in a blog section, a gallery, a team, a FAQ or a contact map from the library, reorder sections by dragging, and delete anything you do not need.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Fill in the page titles and descriptions.</strong> Each page has an SEO title and description field in the Pages tab. A clear title like "Sona One Headphones: Noise Cancelling" beats the default.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
</ol>
