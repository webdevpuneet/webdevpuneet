That is a lot to get right by hand.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A short checklist before you send any email</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Send a test to yourself</strong> and open it on a phone and a desktop, in at least Gmail and one other client.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Replace every placeholder link,</strong> sample name and "yoursite.com" address. The checklist in the editor flags them.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Write the preview text.</strong> The line next to the subject in the inbox is free real estate. Aim for roughly 40 to 130 characters.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Add an unsubscribe link and your postal address</strong> to every marketing email.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Keep the email light.</strong> Short HTML and compressed images stay well under Gmail's limit.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Add alt text to images,</strong> because many people read with images off.</li>

</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Are these HTML email templates really free?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes. You can edit them, copy the HTML and use them for your own business or for clients without paying or signing up. They are part of the free Email Templates library on webdevpuneet.com.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Will they work in Gmail and Outlook?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>They are built for Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, using table-based layouts, inline styles and Outlook-safe buttons, with a responsive layout for phones. Email clients still differ, so always send yourself a test before a big send. Web fonts show in Apple Mail and iOS Mail, while Gmail and Outlook fall back to a similar system font.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">How do I use an email template in Mailchimp or Klaviyo?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose your platform in the editor so the merge tags and unsubscribe link match it, then use Copy HTML and paste the code into a custom-HTML (code your own) email in Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot or Brevo. Upload your images to a host first, or use the ZIP and upload the images folder.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Does the email work in dark mode?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes. The templates include dark-mode styling for clients that support it, such as Apple Mail, iOS Mail and Outlook.com, and the editor has a dark preview so you can check it. Some clients, such as the Gmail app on some devices, apply their own colour changes that no email can fully control.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Why does Gmail say "message clipped"?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gmail cuts off messages larger than about 102 KB and shows a "View entire message" link, which can hide your footer and unsubscribe link. The editor warns you as you approach the limit. Keep copy tight, compress images and avoid pasting huge blocks of code.</p>
